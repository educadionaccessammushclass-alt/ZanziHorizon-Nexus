Transparent School Selection Matrix

Score each criterion from 0 to 5, supported by evidence.

A high score cannot override serious unresolved safeguarding risks. Do not discriminate against schools or learners because of disability or poverty.

Scoring formula

Total score = Sum of (criterion score ÷ 5 × criterion weight). Maximum score: 100.

Pemba Needs Assessment & Baseline Research

Practical donor-ready research framework.

School inspection

Classrooms, safety, desks, WASH, accessibility, learning materials and infrastructure condition.

Head-teacher interview

Enrolment, attendance, staffing, teaching, facilities, learning barriers and existing support.

Teacher questionnaire

Workload, inclusive teaching, specialist support, foundational learning and resources.

Parent consultation

Access barriers, costs, safety, transport, disability inclusion and community priorities.

Learner participation

Safe, voluntary and age-appropriate learner views and experiences.

Disability assessment

Physical, communication, learning and assistive-technology needs.

Information to request from education authorities

  • Current official primary-school register, school codes and locations.
  • Enrolment by school, grade, age and sex, with lawful privacy protections.
  • Attendance, repetition, transfer, dropout, retention and completion records.
  • Teacher establishment, qualifications, vacancies and deployment.
  • Disability and special education data where lawfully available.
  • Infrastructure, WASH, safety and accessibility records.
  • Literacy and numeracy assessment results, with definitions and dates.
  • Existing government and NGO programmes, their coverage and duration.
  • Research permissions, school access requirements and safeguarding pathways.

Evidence classification

VERIFIED

Supported by current official documentation or documented field verification.

HISTORICAL

Previously published information, clearly dated and not presented as current.

ASSUMPTION

Planning estimates or hypotheses awaiting validation.

Head-Teacher Interview Guide

Record responses without unnecessary personal information.

Teacher Questionnaire

Use anonymous teacher reference codes.

Parent & Caregiver Consultation

Voluntary consultation in an accessible language.

Ethical Learner Participation

Obtain required parent or guardian permission and the child's age-appropriate assent. Participation is voluntary. If a child declines, stop. Never publish identifying information.

Disability Inclusion & Assistive Technology

Identify barriers and reasonable accommodation requirements.

Do not diagnose learners. Respect privacy, dignity and the preferences of the learner and family. Seek appropriate specialist advice.

Attendance, Retention, Literacy & Numeracy

Record baseline indicators with a source and reference period.

Attendance rate

Days present ÷ possible attendance days × 100.

Retention

Members of a starting cohort remaining in the defined follow-up cohort, using a consistent definition.

Literacy

Age- and grade-appropriate reading, decoding, accuracy and comprehension measures.

Numeracy

Number recognition, counting, operations, place value and problem-solving measures.
