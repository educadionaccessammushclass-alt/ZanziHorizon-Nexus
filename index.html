Who We Are

Education is the foundation of an inclusive society.

Zanzibar Horizon is an independent initiative designed to advance education access, inclusive learning, research, innovation and sustainable community development.

Our approach begins with understanding the realities of schools and communities, identifying barriers, developing practical interventions and measuring results.

Pemba provides the initial demonstration setting. The long-term architecture is designed to support independently assessed programmes across all 31 regions of Tanzania.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, participate, develop and fulfil their potential.

Implementation Framework

A structured 12-month journey.

The following is a proposed sequence, subject to funding, approvals, access and school-level assessments.

Months 1–2: Preparation and Baseline Assessment

Verify schools, consult communities, assess accessibility, establish baseline indicators and confirm implementation arrangements.

Months 3–4: Planning and Capacity Development

Develop school improvement plans, prioritise interventions and prepare teachers and implementation teams.

Months 5–8: Implementation

Deliver approved school improvements, inclusive learning support, teacher development and appropriate assistive resources.

Months 9–10: Monitoring and Adjustment

Review progress, document challenges, gather feedback and make evidence-based adjustments.

Months 11–12: Evaluation and Reporting

Evaluate results, publish an appropriate report, document lessons and prepare recommendations for future expansion.

Trust and Accountability
